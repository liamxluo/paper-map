Modify mode

Reading List

Lay out what you want to read on a map ordered by year. Each row is a topic or subtopic, each dot is a paper, and you draw the lines yourself: after reading a paper, open it in Modify mode and link the papers it builds on or responds to, so the network grows out of what you have actually read. Green marks “Read”, red marks “Want to read”. Open or close the Contents at the top left. Your data lives in your own private GitHub repository; this page holds no one’s content.

All years

Drag along the year axis to zoom into a period, double-click the map to zoom in on a point, and drag sideways to pan once zoomed in. Pinch or Ctrl + scroll also zooms. Over long spans the axis gives busy decades more room and sparse years less; once you zoom in, it is even.
