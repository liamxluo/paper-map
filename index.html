Builds on Responds to Related to Square: a concept you created Prerequisite concepts (when selected, or always: Settings)

On Builds on and Responds to lines, the arrow points to the later paper, the way ideas flow; Settings → Map can point it at the paper each one builds on instead, or hide it. Related-to lines have no direction, so no arrow.

Drag along the year axis to zoom into a period, double-click the map to zoom in on a point, and drag sideways to pan once zoomed in. Pinch or Ctrl + scroll also zooms. Over long spans the axis gives busy decades more room and sparse years less; once you zoom in, it is even.
